# Glossary

Canonical domain terminology for **TomGee's Scholar Studio**. Use these terms consistently across user interfaces, prompts, documentation, and code.

## Terms

### Agentic Skill
A structured, task-specific scholarly capability invoked within the Scholar Copilot—such as literature synthesis, peer-review critique, claim verification, or Socratic questioning—that combines external literature grounding with active Context Vault evidence.
**Not:** tool, plugin, macro.

### Citation Provenance
The verifiable link between a synthesized claim in a manuscript and the exact source passage or scholarly paper from which it was derived, allowing a researcher to inspect the original context on demand.
**Not:** footnote, raw link.

### Context Vault
A curated collection of uploaded primary source documents, literature excerpts, and saved scholarly papers within a Research Workspace that serves as the factual grounding boundary for synthesis and examination.
**Not:** file list, attachment folder, document cache.

### Examination Session
A structured assessment derived from an Exam Mastery study pack in which a researcher works through a queue of examination questions, receives rubric-based grading against the Context Vault, and tracks mastery scores across questions.
**Not:** quiz popup, flashcard deck.

### Grounded Paper
A peer-reviewed scholarly work retrieved from external academic indexes (such as Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Crossref, or arXiv) with verified bibliographic metadata that can be cited in a manuscript or saved into the Context Vault.
**Not:** web search result, unverified URL.

### Manuscript Canvas
The primary authoring and inspection surface where synthesized research outputs, study packs, and scholarly drafts are read, edited, refined in-place, and prepared for publication or export.
**Not:** preview pane, output box, text area.

### Research Workspace
An isolated project environment containing its own Context Vault documents, Manuscript Canvas draft history, and Scholar Copilot inquiry thread, dedicated to a specific thesis, course, or research topic.
**Not:** folder, tab, user profile.

### Scholar Copilot
The conversational research collaborator that answers complex inquiries, runs Agentic Skills, surfaces Grounded Papers, and inserts verified prose directly into the Manuscript Canvas.
**Not:** chatbot, assistant widget.

### Vault Snapshot
A self-contained, portable archive of a researcher's workspaces, Context Vault documents, draft history, and scholarly conversations that can be exported for backup or restored across devices.
**Not:** database dump, cache export.

### Viva Voce Probe
An adversarial follow-up challenge issued by the Academic Examiner after grading an Examination Session response, testing whether the researcher can defend their mechanism or argument under boundary conditions or counter-evidence.
**Not:** hint, retry prompt.
