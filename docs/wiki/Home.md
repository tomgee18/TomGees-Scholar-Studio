**TomGee's Scholar Studio** is a local-first, offline-capable academic research suite and study engine designed for graduate researchers, doctoral scholars, and high-stakes examination candidates. Packaged for native desktop execution with **Tauri v2** and built on a high-performance **React 19** frontend.
